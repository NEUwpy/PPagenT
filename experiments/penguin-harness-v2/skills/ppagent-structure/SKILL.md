# PPagenT Structure

Use a Structure Group only when the relationship itself matters: comparison, parallel, sequence, hierarchy, cycle, matrix, branch, or network. Inspect the candidate contract and preview before selecting. Preserve the declared polarity, order, connector semantics, Content Frame, and minimum readable type.

Allowed adaptation is limited to the candidate contract: content, count, declared spacing, and declared visual parameters. A run-local derived variant must not alter the core asset or silently become a new registered Structure Group.
